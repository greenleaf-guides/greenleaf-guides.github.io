Formulating the optimal growing substrate in a Cold Frame Bed for Deep Root Vegetables & Taproot Drainage Ratio requires the correct physical balance between drainage porosity and water retention.

The Proven 60:30:10 Volumetric Standard

  • 60% Quality Screened Topsoil: Provides heavy mineral anchor and moisture buffering.
  • 30% Organic Aged Compost: Sustains biological microbial activity and steady nitrogen release.
  • 10% Aeration Minerals (Perlite / Coarse Sand): Prevents compaction and enables capillary oxygenation.
