When to Plant Cabbage in USDA Zone 5

← Back to Master Botanical Index

Growing Cabbage successfully in USDA Zone 5 requires calculating the precise window between the average final spring frost and the first autumn freeze.

Zone 5 Timetable & Thermal Requirements

  • Indoor Seed Starting: 6 to 8 weeks before average last spring frost date.
  • Hardening Off: 7 to 10 days of gradual ambient exposure once night temperatures stabilize.
  • Soil Temperature: Minimum 55°F (13°C) for active root development; optimal 65°F-75°F.

Transplanting prematurely into cold ground causes root stunting, phosphorus lockout, and leaf chlorosis. Consistent soil warmth and organic mulch ensure rapid establishment.
